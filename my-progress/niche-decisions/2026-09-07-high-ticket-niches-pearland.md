national franchises — Groundworks, Olshan), pivoted to find niches that combine a genuine
$5k+ per-job ticket with no national franchise saturation. See
[`high-ticket-niche-candidates` reasoning](#reasoning) below and the

## Reasoning

The pattern found in foundation repair (Richmond/Pearland/Spring all showing Groundworks
and/or Olshan) suggested the *category itself* — not the town — was the problem: any niche
attractive enough to draw a national roll-up (foundation, roofing, HVAC install, solar, pool,
water damage, mold remediation) tends to already be saturated everywhere. High ticket value is
part of *why* those categories get bought up by chains.

The filter that actually matters for a first microsite: **high ticket + no dominant national
franchise yet.** Categories that are hyper-local/permit-driven (septic) or too fragmented/
low-margin-per-job for a franchise roll-up to bother with (tree removal, fencing, driveway
paving) fit that filter better than the "big ticket" categories that first come to mind.

## Recommendation

1. **Septic System Installation** is the strongest pick: genuine $5k+ tickets, zero franchise
   risk found, and structurally hard for any chain to templatize (local permitting, soil
   testing, county health department rules vary site to site).
2. Runner-up: **Fence Installation** — reliable $5k+ on full-yard jobs, clean competitive
   picture, easier build (no permit dependency).
3. **Driveway Paving** — good ticket size but meaningfully more existing local supply to
   out-rank (~98 Pearland contractors on one directory alone).
4. **Tree Removal** — only hits the $5k+ bar if positioned for large/storm-damage jobs
   specifically, not routine tree trimming/removal.
