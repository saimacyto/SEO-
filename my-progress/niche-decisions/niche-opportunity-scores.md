# Niche Opportunity Scoring Matrix

A first-pass opportunity ranking across ~100 local-service niches, used to shortlist
candidates before running any of them through the Niche Validator (see
[Step 1](../../academy/01-first-1000/microsite-playbook-step1-niche-and-town.md)).

**Columns (inferred from the data — column 4 = column 1 + column 2 + column 3 in every row):**

| Column | Working label | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Ticket/urgency value | Higher = bigger payday per lead / more urgent for the customer |
| B | Opportunity (low competition) | Higher = less brand loyalty / more winnable for a small site |
| C | Saturation penalty | Negative; bigger magnitude = more national/franchise/PPC pressure in this category |
| D | **Total score** | A + B + C — the ranking number, higher is better |

This is a **pre-screen**, not a substitute for Step 1. Treat a high total as "worth spending a
Niche Validator lookup on," not as a green light to build. Still confirm with the Validator +
manual Google quote-search per niche+town combo before committing.

## Reading this against Step 1's rules

- **Top of the list** (Emergency Locksmith, Roofing Repair, Towing Service, Emergency
  Electrician, Auto Locksmith, Plumbing, Roadside Assistance, Tree Removal, Septic
  Tank/Inspection) pair high urgency with genuinely low brand loyalty — exactly the "nobody has
  a favorite company for a burst water line" profile the playbook wants.
- **Foundation Repair lands mid-pack at 7** (tied with ~10 other niches, roughly position
  50-60 of 100): high ticket value (A=10) but low opportunity (B=5) and a heavy saturation
  penalty (C=-8) — consistent with the concern already logged in
  [`2026-09-07-foundation-dallas.md`](./2026-09-07-foundation-dallas.md) about established
  regional players and brand loyalty in this category. Not disqualifying on its own, but a
  weaker starting point than several higher-scoring alternatives above it — worth validating a
  couple of top-10 niches from this list in parallel before committing to foundation repair.
- **Bottom of the list** (Solar Panel Installation, Pool Installation, General Contractor, Lawn
  Care) pair low opportunity with heavy saturation/penalty — high-competition categories to
  avoid for a first site regardless of ticket size.
