# Nationwide High-Ticket Niche Scan (Not Location-Specific)

Broadened from the Texas/Houston-metro search after the "foundation repair" pattern showed
that categories attractive enough for a $5k+ ticket often get bought up by national roll-up
franchises (Groundworks, Olshan, ServPro, Roto-Rooter, solar/pool installers). This is a
nationwide brainstorm of niches that hit **high ticket ($5k+) + genuinely fragmented, no
dominant national franchise** — the actual filter that matters, established in
[`2026-09-07-high-ticket-niches-pearland.md`](./2026-09-07-high-ticket-niches-pearland.md).

**Method:** general knowledge + targeted web research on franchise presence per category —
NOT yet run through the Niche Validator or a per-town manual check. Treat every entry here as
"worth validating," not "validated."

## Categories to avoid (known national franchise/roll-up saturation)

Foundation repair (Groundworks, Olshan), water damage/mold restoration (ServPro, Servicemaster,
Rainbow International, Paul Davis), solar installation (Sunrun, Tesla, ADT Solar), pool
installation, HVAC installation (large dealer networks), pest control (Terminix, Orkin), home
security (ADT, Vivint), moving (Two Men and a Truck, U-Haul, PODS), carpet cleaning (Stanley
Steemer), junk removal (1-800-GOT-JUNK, College Hunks), window/siding/gutters in many markets
(Window World, Renewal by Andersen, LeafFilter), basement waterproofing (often the same
Groundworks-affiliated companies as foundation repair).

## Strongest picks (checked for franchise risk this round)

1. **Water Well Drilling & Installation** — $8k-$25k+ per well.
   **Verified:** ~8,400 water well drilling businesses in the US (IBISWorld), genuinely
   fragmented industry. No dominant national residential franchise — Layne Christensen operates
   at industrial/municipal scale, not a residential local-SEO competitor. Applies in any region
   with private well usage (rural TX, Southeast, Northeast, Pacific NW, etc.).
   **Top pick — cleanest confirmed no-franchise, high-ticket category found.**

2. **Agricultural/Livestock Fencing** (high-tensile, game fence, large-acreage) — $10k-$50k+
   per property. Distinct trade from residential fence installation; rural/ag-state focused;
   no national brand identified.

3. **Metal Building / Pole Barn Erection** — $20k-$100k+ per structure. Rural/ag states,
   fragmented local builders, no national franchise identified.

4. **ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) Construction** — $50k-$150k+. Newer/fast-growing category;
   many mid-size markets still thin on SEO competition. Longer sales cycle — likely fits the
   "lead sale to builder" monetization route better than a monthly-lease model.

## Good, but verify per-market before committing

5. **Storm Shelter / Safe Room Installation** — $4k-$10k+, tornado-belt states (TX/OK/KS/NE/
   AR/MO). Fragmented at the local-installer level, but manufacturers (Atlas Safe Rooms, F5
   Storm Shelters, FamilySAFE, Vault Pro, National Storm Shelters, The Turner Co) run their own
   aggressive national SEO/PPC and may already occupy some city+niche search results — check
   the target market before committing.

6. **Impact/Hurricane Window & Door Installation** — $10k-$30k, coastal/storm states.
   Fragmented at the installer level, though overlaps some dealer-network brands (Window World,
   Renewal by Andersen) in certain regions.

## Downgraded after checking — more franchise risk than expected

7. **Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement** — looked promising (broader addressable market than
   septic since it applies to any sewered city with aging infrastructure), but **Roto-Rooter is
   a genuine national franchise** already running dedicated "sewer line replacement" /
   "trenchless" pages per city (confirmed a New Jersey-specific example). Same franchise-risk
   pattern as foundation repair — only worth it in a specific market where Roto-Rooter doesn't
   already dominate that search.
