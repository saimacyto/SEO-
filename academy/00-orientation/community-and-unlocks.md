# Mindset, Community, and How Unlocks Work

## Mindset

Have fun, explore, make friends, learn. Rome wasn't built in a day — nothing needs to be
finished today, tomorrow, or the next day.

> "I'm going to better myself today however big or however small."

Even tiny progress counts. There's no single straight path to success — everyone learns and
builds a bit differently. What matters is making the leap to improve today.

## Beyond the classroom

The classroom is the obvious part of the community, but the calls, the lessons that grow out
of them, and the things that unlock along the way matter just as much.

## Why some things are locked

Two kinds of resources:

1. **Standard resources** — both playbooks (Upwork, Microsite), the Microsite Builder, the
   Niche Validator, the Offer Library, the AI workflows, the weekly calls, and daily support in
   the comments.
2. **Premium resources** — Microsite Empire (contains the Blueprint), Agency Engine, and the
   Backlink Rolodex.

Every Standard member eventually gets the premium resources too — they open as membership
ages, on a monthly schedule:

- **Microsite Empire** — unlocks at 30 days
- **Agency Engine** — unlocks at 60 days
- **The Backlink Rolodex** — opens up more each month after month 1

Not tied to plan at all:

- **Call Vault** — opens for everyone once the trial ends
- **Wisdom's In The Calls** — opens at Level 3 for everyone (see below)

**Annual members** get everything open on day one (part of the annual deal — annual works out
to 2 months free vs. monthly). **Monthly members** get everything needed to land a first
client immediately; premium resources just arrive on the schedule above rather than being
withheld.

## How levels work

- Every like another member gives your post or comment = 1 point.
- Points raise your level; level shows on your avatar.
- Leveling up = the same behavior that gets the most out of the community anyway: share what
  you're building, ask real questions, answer someone else's question (answering counts as
  much as posting).

## Wisdom's In The Calls (unlocks at Level 3)

Weekly live calls share what's actually working right now, but live calls are hard to search —
a good tactic gets buried in a recording nobody can find later. The best insights from calls
get pulled out, written up as standalone lessons, and credited to the member who shared them.
That library is **Wisdom's In The Calls**, unlocked at **Level 3 = 20 likes received**
(a couple weeks of showing up in the comments tends to get you there).

## Off the Record (earned off the leaderboard, not levels)

Once a month, an unrecorded call — no replay, no clips, nothing leaves the room — for real
numbers, real client situations, what's being tested next. Invite works one way: on the last
day of each month, the 30-day leaderboard is taken as-is, and everyone on it gets a DM. No
applications, no favorites — the leaderboard decides. Leaderboard runs on the same points as
levels: one like on a post or comment = one point.

## Bonus resources

New tools, workflows, and courses ship regularly. Some land for everyone right away; others
show up as unlocks/bonuses over time. Every community call is recorded into the **Call Vault**,
so nothing shared on a call is ever lost.

## The short version

Everything extra is earned the same way: being part of the community. Post what you're
building, ask when stuck, help when you can — the unlocks take care of themselves.
