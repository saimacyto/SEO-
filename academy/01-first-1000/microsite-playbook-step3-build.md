# Microsite Playbook — Step 3: Build the Site with the One-Click Builder

This is the core of the whole module: turning your niche and location into a real, structured
site. You're not going to build it by hand — you're going to use the **One-Click Builder**, a
tool built for exactly this. Answer a few questions, generate content with a custom AI tuned
for the job, and the Builder hands you a finished site, ready to publish. No code, no platform
to manage, no theme to wrestle with.

## Steps

1. Go to [build.rankexpand.com](https://build.rankexpand.com) (or use the downloadable
   builder). Access code: `PLAYBOOK2026`.
2. Tell the Builder about the business: primary service, services (1 to 6), primary location,
   service-area towns, and the basics — name, phone, brand color, logo if you have one.
3. Generate your content: the Builder hands you a one-click link to your content generator.
   Open it in ChatGPT, paste the pre-formatted details the Builder gives you, and it writes
   every page in one pass.
4. Load your content into the Builder: paste or drop the file back in. Every page's copy lands
   in the right place automatically.
5. Add your photos (optional): upload one hero image and a few in-content photos. The Builder
   bundles them into the site and spreads them through your pages.
6. Download your finished site: a clean folder of static HTML, ready to upload to any host.
7. Save your project file: the Builder gives you a project file with everything in it. Keep
   it — later, when you add call tracking, change your phone number, or update anything,
   reload that file, make the change, and re-download. No starting over.

## Why 1-6 services, not more

A microsite wins by being focused. One service ranked hard across a real area beats a site
trying to be everything. More services you're excited about? That's your next site.

## Review and tighten

The generator writes a strong first draft, but it's a draft. AI gets you 90% there; the last
10% makes it read like a real local business:

- Fix the facts.
- Add one real detail per page.
- Cut anything generic.
- Aim for true and specific, not perfect.

**Important workflow note:** generate the site first, then take the output HTML files and feed
those to your AI to edit the text. Avoid editing the initial JSON content file directly —
editing HTML output, not the JSON, avoids errors.

## Out of scope for a first site

Customizing the build system itself, running it across dozens of sites, and power-tool setups
at scale are operator-level work covered in Owner OS. For your first site, the Builder is all
you need.
