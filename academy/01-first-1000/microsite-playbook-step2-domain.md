## The five rules

1. **`.com` only.**
2. **Exact match domains only.** A branded name like `locksmithpro.com` holds no SEO value in
   this method. You can still rank it, you just give up the advantage the domain exists to
   provide.
3. **Vary by location or by intent, never by word order.** `PlumbersHouston.com` and
   `HoustonPlumbers.com` chase the same intent, so the second one is the same site bought
   twice. `PlumbersHouston.com` and `PlumbersDallas.com` is the version that works.
4. **One intent and one geographic footprint per website.** That is the governing rule, and it
   is also the cleanest description of what a microsite is.
5. **Use the pattern `[service][location].com`.**
