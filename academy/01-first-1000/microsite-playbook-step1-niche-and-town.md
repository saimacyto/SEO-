# Microsite Playbook — Step 1: Pick a Service and a Town

You need two things: a service, and a town.

## Step 1. Talk it through with the Niche Finder GPT

It asks about where you live and what you already know, and suggests niches worth
investigating. No interface to learn. Start here even if you already think you have an idea —
the good niches are usually the ones you haven't thought of.

➡️ [Niche Finder GPT](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6a71c2769acc8191bbac3e3b98db731e-rank-expand-niche-finder)

## Step 2. Check your shortlist in the Niche Validator

Go to the [Niche Validator](http://tool.rankexpand.com/members) and use access code
`NICHECHECK`. As an Academy member you get 15 lookups a day — three is usually enough for a
first site. Put in the two or three combinations the GPT gave you and compare.

➡️ [Niche Validator 🔑](http://tool.rankexpand.com/members) — access code `NICHECHECK`

## Step 3. Sanity check it by hand

Put your keyword plus location in quote marks in the Google search box and look at how many
competing pages come back.

Example: `"concrete contractor modesto ca"` returned 29 competitors, 90 searches a month, in a
city of 219,000. **That is a build.**

## Three things that stop people

1. **The map pack is not the metric.** Almost every local search has one now. One member
   checked fifteen locations across fifteen services each, and every single one had a map
   pack. Almost all of our own sites compete against one and pull leads fine. Finding a search
   without one is a leg up, not a requirement.
2. **Do not chase low search volume away.** A keyword plus an area with little reported volume
   is still fine — volume tools do not see everything, and one job from a plumber pays for the
   site many times over.
3. **One service, one area, one site.** That is the shape. If you're excited about a second
   service, that's your second site, not more pages on this one.

## Stop second-guessing

The analysis has a floor, and past it you're just delaying. Pick something high ticket, low
competition, where nobody has brand loyalty — nobody has a favorite company for a burst water
line. A live, mediocre site teaches you more in two weeks than another month of spreadsheets,
and a wrong pick costs you an afternoon.

**Do this now:** run the GPT, validate two or three, pick one. Post what you picked in the
community and get feedback.
