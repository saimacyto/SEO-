# Suggested Path — Picking Upwork vs. Microsite

Two ways to earn your first $1,000 inside Rank Expand Academy. They both work — they suit
different people. Here's how to pick yours in under a minute.

## Default: The Upwork Playbook

Start here unless you have a reason not to — it's the default for most members.

- Set up an Upwork profile, pick deliverables from the Offer Library, send applications, land
  your first paying client.
- No upfront tool costs to start.
- Fastest path to first dollar.

If you want speed and you're willing to pitch every weekday: open the Upwork Playbook and
start with Day 1.

## Alternative: The Microsite Playbook

Pick this if you've tried freelancing and hated it, or you'd rather own an asset than sell your
time.

- Build one ranking microsite, then monetize it — leasing to a local business, affiliate
  revenue, or running ads on it.
- Slower path to first dollar (typically 30–60 days), but you own the asset at the end.

If you want to build once and earn repeatedly: open the Microsite Playbook.

## The Offer Library is a catalog, not a path

It lists the deliverables and AI workflows you pitch on either path above. Open it when you're
ready to choose what to actually ship — not before you've picked a path. Both playbooks point
you there at the right moment; no need to start there.

## Where both paths lead

Both paths lead to the same place: your first paying client or your first lead deal. From
there, a different kind of work begins — turning one client into a client base, or one site
into a portfolio. That's Owner OS territory (see [Module 1](./README.md#two-rooms-two-jobs)):

- **Agency Engine** — for Upwork-path members building a service business.
- **Microsite Empire** — for microsite-path members scaling into a portfolio.

Not needed yet — pick a path here, hit your first $1k, then you'll be pointed to Owner OS.

## Still not sure?

Don't agonize over the choice. Default to the Upwork Playbook, give it 30 days, and switch to
the Microsite Playbook if you're not landing clients. **The only mistake is paralysis.**
