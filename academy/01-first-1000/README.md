# Module 1 — Getting to Your First $1,000 Online

Rank Expand Academy is built around one outcome: **getting you to your first $1,000 online.**
Every module is designed for that — templates you can ship, AI workflows you can sell,
playbooks you can follow today. The whole product is engineered to take someone with a real
skill (or someone willing to learn one fast) from zero to first paying client.

## Two paths to first $1k

- **The Upwork Playbook** — find someone who'll pay for SEO and AI work today, deliver it,
  get paid.
- **The Microsite Playbook** — build a ranking microsite, lease it or sell its leads.

## The catalog you draw from

- **The Offer Library** — productized deliverables you can sell on either path.

## The toolkit that powers it

- **AI Tools & Workflows** — custom GPTs and n8n workflows that produce the deliverables.
- **Outreach at Scale** — cold outreach systems for finding clients beyond Upwork.

**Structure:** pick a path, use the catalog and toolkit to produce what you sell.

## Two rooms, two jobs

There's a second program, **Owner OS**, that picks up where this one ends — for members who
already have some wins and want to build the systems to scale. The rooms are kept separate on
purpose:

- **This room (Rank Expand Academy)** — getting paid: deliverables, workflows, playbooks.
- **The other room (Owner OS)** — building the business. Three modules: Owner OS (the
  operating system), Microsite Empire (portfolio scaling), Agency Engine (the recurring
  service business).

You don't need Owner OS to start. Most members won't need it until they've hit their first
$1k and are deciding whether to turn it into a recurring business — pointed to it at the
right moment.

Go to **Suggested Path** to pick which playbook (Upwork or Microsite) to start with.
