# The Upwork Playbook

The fastest path to your first paying client.

You set up an Upwork profile, pick deliverables from the Offer Library, send applications, and
land your first paying client — usually within 2 to 4 weeks of consistent effort. Costs
nothing upfront. Most members start here.

## Pick this if

- You want speed — first dollar in weeks, not months.
- You're willing to pitch every weekday for the first month.
- You have $0 to invest upfront.
- You'd rather have a client tell you what to build than guess what will rank.

## Skip this if

- You've tried freelancing platforms before and hated the race-to-the-bottom dynamics.
- You'd rather build an asset you own than sell your time. (→ see
  [Suggested Path](./suggested-path.md) and the [Microsite Playbook](./microsite-playbook.md)
  instead.)

## What you'll learn inside the playbook

- How to set up an Upwork profile that gets noticed in a crowded market.
- How to pitch work without racing to the lowest price.
- How to pick deliverables you can ship from the Offer Library.
- How to send applications consistently for 30 days until clients come to you.
- How to turn a first job into a repeat client or referral.
